# Journal and story positioning

## CONSERVATIVE

- Results needed: valid primary analyses even if null, modest or dispersion-qualified; transparent sensitivity grid and strong reproducibility contribution.
- Blocking weakness: unresolved runtime/input QC, incomplete primary cohort, or a story dependent on unexecuted DA.
- Framing: public-data ecological measurement study; two separate clinical examples; methods and interpretive boundaries lead.
- Likely objections: descriptive synthesis, classifier dependence, absent common covariates, no controls in the external cohort.
- Scientific fit: microbiome methods, research data, pulmonary microbiology or focused infectious-disease journals.

## REALISTIC

- Results needed: at least one coherent cohort-specific primary effect, interpretable dispersion, stable effect scale over prespecified filters/zero methods, and informative secondary ecology.
- Blocking weakness: effect only under one threshold, classified-fraction confounding that explains the pattern, or incompatible Aitchison/Bray results without a clear account.
- Framing: clinical-state-associated lower-airway community organization under a common classifier grammar; effect size and heterogeneity emphasized.
- Likely objections: different clinical estimands, observational confounding, classifier-defined subcomposition, limited contamination controls.
- Scientific fit: specialist microbiome, respiratory infection, translational microbiology or pulmonary research journals.

## AMBITIOUS

- Results needed: material and robust community organization in both cohorts, defensible dispersion behavior, coherent secondary effects, strong complete-method provenance and a synthesis that teaches something beyond two separate P values.
- Blocking weakness: null/trivial effects, material PERMDISP qualification throughout, unstable feature spaces, or reliance on taxon claims without controls.
- Framing: cross-cohort ecological architecture and generalizability of community-level measurement—not a replicated disease signature.
- Likely objections: only two cohorts, nonexchangeable contrasts, no formal effect synthesis, low classified fractions, and lack of a true external negative control.
- Scientific fit: broader microbial ecology or respiratory journals; suitability should be judged by scope and evidentiary depth rather than an unverified current impact factor.
