# PRJNA977832 / SRP440548 cohort status

**Status: `PARKED_RUN_TO_HIV_MAPPING_UNRESOLVED`**

The paper reports 756 participants (HIV-positive 476; HIV-negative 280),
whereas the public inventory contains 718 runs. The 756-to-718 reconciliation
is unresolved, and none of the 718 public runs has a deterministic accession-
level HIV label or auditable participant/specimen bridge. The deposited reads
are upstream processed/human-subtracted rather than native raw reads. Full
acquisition is therefore not justified without a new public or author-provided
run-to-HIV mapping. The ENA gzip representation totals 504,633,902,336 bytes
(about 470.0 GiB); the NCBI SRA archive/container metric is
985,003,982,848 bytes (about 917.36 GiB). These are different storage
representations, not contradictory dataset sizes.

No further metadata rescue is planned under the current scope. Reactivation
requires deterministic run-to-HIV mapping and public-subset inclusion
provenance. No raw reads were downloaded and no biological analysis was run.
