# Parent-study and batch-confounding assessment

**PARENT_STUDY_CONFOUNDED_WITH_DISEASE=true.** The paper explicitly places all 100 healthy controls in MicroCOPD and all 57 ILD cases in MicroILD. Consequently, parent study is perfectly aligned with healthy-versus-ILD case status. This is a major design risk for any disease-attribution claim even though collection was in one centre by the same investigators.

**BATCH_CONFOUNDED_WITH_DISEASE=unresolved.** The paper provides common extraction, library and instrument descriptions, but does not publish sequencing dates, lanes, library plate IDs, technical batches or a sample manifest. Perfect batch confounding is neither proved nor excluded.

**COLLECTION_PROTOCOL_COMPARABLE=partial.** The site, protective sheath, oral route, no-suction-before-carina practice, centre and investigators are common; however disease subjects received 3 x 50 ml PBAL whereas controls received 2 x 50 ml. The difference in collection volume prevents an unqualified claim of identical collection protocol.

Age, sex and smoking are reported/used in the paper's PERMANOVA adjustment, but the sample-level metadata and batch variables needed to assess residual confounding have not been publicly released.
