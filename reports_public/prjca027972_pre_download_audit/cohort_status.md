# PRJCA027972 / OMIX006862 cohort status

**Status: `METHODS_ONLY_RESERVE`**

This is a retention status, not a deletion or scientific-invalidity judgment. Preserve the existing audit evidence and do not download the 2.9-GB archive for the lower-airway cross-cohort ecology programme.

## Basis

- The paper cohort has 66 patients, but the portal’s “138 samples” is not a valid participant count.
- The release contains 90 Illumina host-depleted FASTA and 66 Nanopore FASTA records.
- A deterministic, universal subject/specimen bridge across the two platforms remains unresolved.
- Illumina files are processed `*.nohost.fasta`, not native raw reads.
- A clinically independent, non-circular ecological estimand has not been demonstrated from the public evidence.

It may be reconsidered as a technical or platform-methods resource if a documented subject/specimen/platform mapping and independent clinical labels become available. No raw data were downloaded and no biological analysis was executed.
