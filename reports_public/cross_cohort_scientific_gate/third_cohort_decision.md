# Third-cohort decision

## Decision: OPTIONAL

A third cohort is **not required for the viability of the current manuscript** if the manuscript is framed as two independent within-cohort clinical association analyses connected by a common ecological measurement layer and a qualified cross-cohort synthesis. The verified 400+130 reports are sufficient to test the anchor four-diagnosis omnibus and the external TB drug-resistance contrast separately.

A third cohort would be useful for a stronger future claim. In particular, genuine replication would require a cohort that repeats either the four-diagnosis anchor contrast or the drug-resistant versus drug-sensitive TB contrast with deterministic labels, comparable eligibility/specimen collection, adequate covariates and compatible raw/classifier provenance. Such a cohort could support prespecified replication and, only if the estimand were truly common, formal effect synthesis.

The current study should not delay analysis merely to increase cohort count, and it should not search for or select another cohort post hoc to obtain concordant signs. No additional cohort reconnaissance is authorized or performed by this gate.
