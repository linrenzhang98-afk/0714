# Candidate estimands

## Candidate primary estimand

**CAP versus severe-pneumonia-associated lower-airway community variation, within the Luo BALF cohort.**

Status: **SUPPORTED as a paper-level question; UNRESOLVED as an accession-level analysis estimand.** The paper reports the groups, but there is no verified run/BioSample-to-subject-to-group map.

This is not a replication of the PRJNA1056765 four-diagnosis effect. It is not commensurate with PRJCA046985 drug-resistant versus drug-sensitive tuberculosis as one pooled disease effect.

## Admissible future role, if mapping closes

The cohort may provide a cohort-specific clinical grouping / severity-associated community analysis, followed by comparable ecological summaries (alpha diversity, dominance, classified fraction, Aitchison beta diversity, PERMANOVA R² and PERMDISP) with separate interpretation. It must not be pooled into one cross-study abundance matrix or disease-effect estimate.

## Design candidates for adversarial adjudication

1. Unadjusted descriptive CAP/severe contrasts, with exact group counts and uncertainty.
2. A prespecified minimal baseline-confounder model limited to verified pre-exposure factors (candidate: age, sex, selected pre-existing comorbidities), only if overlap and sample linkage permit it.
3. Sensitivity analyses that separately describe severity-score, ventilation, treatment, antibiotic timing, and batch limitations rather than blindly conditioning on them.
4. No primary differential-abundance claim until mapping, covariate timing, compositional choices, multiplicity control, and rare-taxon rules are frozen.

The severe group has n=25. Any future inference needs finite-sample-aware permutations, constrained/restricted permutations where appropriate, dispersion testing, effect sizes and uncertainty, and careful treatment of prevalence/rarity.
