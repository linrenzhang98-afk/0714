# Unsent author/data-curation request: PRJCA039020 / CRA024916 / PRJDB36521

Dear corresponding and data-curation authors,

We are performing a reproducibility review of the de-identified BALF metagenomic cohort reported in Luo et al., *Microbial and clinical disparities in pneumonia* (DOI: 10.3389/fmicb.2025.1538109). We will not request any identifiable patient information.

Could you please provide, or point us to, a de-identified mapping table that links each GSA/INSDC run or BioSample accession (for example DRR/DRX, SAMD/SAMC, DRS, or `BALF_###`) to:

- a stable de-identified study participant/sample ID;
- inclusion in the final 229-patient analysis; and
- the published CAP or severe-pneumonia group label.

The public deposits contain 233 records, whereas the paper’s final analysis contains 204 CAP and 25 severe-pneumonia patients. Please also identify the role of the four additional public records (for example, excluded subject, technical replicate, control, or another reason).

The paper describes 243 originally sequenced pneumonia specimens (218 CAP and 25 severe pneumonia) and exclusion of 14 CAP cases for incomplete clinical data. If the 233 public records are a subset of those 243 specimens, could you clarify why ten sequenced cases are not public and, if feasible, provide their de-identified inclusion/deposit status?

This information would allow an auditable replication of the published cohort definition without any patient re-identification.

Sincerely,

0714 reproducibility review team
