# Editorial summary v2

The draft was rewritten around five positive results: a very small conditional diagnosis component, pseudocount stability within the 30-species space, stronger feature-space dependence, dispersion-sensitive full-cohort Bray–Curtis results, and representation-dependent community heterogeneity.

Repeated defensive language was consolidated into one boundary statement per major section. Engineering provenance moved from the main Methods to Supplementary Methods and Data and code availability. The Discussion now distinguishes effect size from significance, explains PERMANOVA/PERMDISP, places CLR and zero replacement in compositional context, addresses low-biomass QC and database dependence, and interprets clustering stability without proposing ecotypes.

The preferred title is “Compositional robustness reveals small diagnosis-associated variation and continuous heterogeneity in BALF metagenomes.” It avoids dataset-accession and audit-report phrasing while preserving the bounded scientific claim. Four alternatives remain in the manuscript for later editorial choice.

The reference set expanded from 10 to 31 verified, high-relevance papers. Every external methodological or biological statement is mapped to a numbered citation; frozen numerical results map to a figure, table, or source-data file.
