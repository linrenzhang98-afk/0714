# Key references

## Compositional geometry and zeros

- Aitchison J. *The Statistical Analysis of Compositional Data*. 1982. Foundational simplex/log-ratio framework. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1982.tb01195.x)
- Gloor GB et al. *Microbiome Datasets Are Compositional: And This Is Not Optional*. 2017. Microbiome-specific compositional interpretation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2017.02224)
- Palarea-Albaladejo J, Martín-Fernández JA. *zCompositions—R package for multivariate imputation of left-censored data under a compositional approach*. 2015. Correct zCompositions paper and DOI. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemolab.2015.02.019)
- Martín-Fernández JA et al. *Dealing with zeros and missing values in compositional data sets using nonparametric imputation*. 2003. Multiplicative-replacement context. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1023866030544)

## Community inference and dispersion

- Anderson MJ. *A new method for non-parametric multivariate analysis of variance*. 2001. PERMANOVA formulation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1442-9993.2001.01070.pp.x)
- Anderson MJ. *Distance-Based Tests for Homogeneity of Multivariate Dispersions*. 2006. PERMDISP formulation. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1541-0420.2005.00440.x)

- Nearing JT et al. *Microbiome differential abundance methods produce different results across 38 datasets*. 2022. Method-disagreement risk. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28034-z)

The audit is bounded: these sources justify methods and interpretive limits; it is not an exhaustive pulmonary microbiome review. The zCompositions paper was externally rechecked because a superficially plausible DOI (`10.1002/cem.2730`) resolves to an unrelated soil-spectrometry article and must not be cited.
