# Cross-cohort analysis plan

## Level 1: within-cohort estimates

Each cohort is a separate study and estimand. Before viewing community results, freeze eligible subjects, raw diagnosis mapping, one-sample-per-subject rule, covariates, permutation restrictions, feature definitions, zero replacement, distances, and QC sensitivities. Report n, group counts, retained feature count, diagnosis-associated PERMANOVA R² with uncertainty where feasible, permutation P as secondary, PERMDISP statistic/P, and cluster stability only for adequately sized cohorts. The anchor's frozen v5 design remains unchanged.

The same taxonomy database is desirable for the reanalysis, but identical classification does not make specimens, centres, diagnoses, or preprocessing exchangeable. Covariates are used only when estimable within a cohort. A study term cannot rescue a design in which diagnosis is perfectly confounded with study.

## Level 2: cross-cohort synthesis

Build a cohort-estimate table containing study, specimen, clinical contrast, n, feature space, metric, pseudocount, R², uncertainty method, PERMDISP qualification, and cluster diagnostics. The primary synthesis is descriptive: forest-style R² display grouped by estimand class, dispersion status, and qualitative consistency of feature-space/pseudocount behavior. Do not interpret overlapping or non-overlapping P values as replication.

## Why R² is not automatically meta-analysed

PERMANOVA R² depends on distance geometry, group allocation, covariates, dispersion, feature space, and sample composition. Its sampling distribution and precision are not supplied by the point estimate alone, and these cohorts target nonidentical contrasts. Inverse-variance meta-analysis is therefore not prespecified. A formal model could be considered later only if at least three cohorts estimate a meaningfully common contrast, uncertainty is generated by a frozen within-cohort resampling scheme, weights are justified, heterogeneity is estimable, and DeepSeek approves the transformation/model. Until then, a stratified forest display without pooled diamond is safer.

## Pooled models

Concatenating abundance matrices and testing diagnosis is prohibited as a primary analysis. Any future pooled sensitivity requires explicit approval and an estimable design with study/centre/batch terms, within-study permutation or stratification, study-by-diagnosis interaction assessment, and proof that diagnosis is not aliased with study. Batch correction must never remove a biological variable that is confounded with study.
