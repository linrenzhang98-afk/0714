# Robustness claims after frozen v5

## Supported

- The frozen full-cohort 10% P1 Aitchison result was reproduced exactly from the locked inputs and executable.
- Within the frozen 30-species feature space, the omnibus R² estimate was insensitive to the two prespecified pseudocounts: 0.0194095 versus 0.0190746.
- The full-cohort 30-species Aitchison variance component was very small and was not dispersion-qualified under either pseudocount.
- Prespecified feature-space, metric, and QC-population choices materially changed the effect-size estimate or its dispersion qualification. This is a robustness finding about the frozen pipeline, not a biological disease finding.
- Full-cohort Bray–Curtis results require location-and/or-dispersion language at every prespecified prevalence threshold.

## Pipeline-dependent only

- The n=119 strict-QC results describe only a pipeline-dependent sensitivity population. They do not provide an independent test of the full-cohort result or a biologically privileged population.
- The larger strict-QC R² values at 5% and 10% cannot be interpreted as strengthening diagnosis effects because the population and estimand changed.
- The near-zero 20% Aitchison estimates arise in a two-species feature space and cannot adjudicate the biological relevance of diagnosis.
- Taxon overlap or mismatch with Han et al. can be described only as pipeline/statistical concordance or discrepancy. It cannot be interpreted as biological agreement or disagreement while upstream database, negative-control handling, filtering, normalization, and feature definitions remain unresolved.

## Not supported

- A robust disease fingerprint, disease-specific community subtype, biomarker, diagnostic claim, causal mechanism, or taxon-level discovery.
- Pairwise conclusions about cancer, tuberculosis, bacterial infection, or fungal infection.
- A single pooled sensitivity range across non-poolable audit layers.
- Choosing a preferred cell according to significance, effect size, or dispersion behavior.
