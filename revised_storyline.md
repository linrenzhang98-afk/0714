# Revised storyline

## Working question

How much diagnosis-associated lower-airway community variation remains interpretable after separating compositional location, multivariate dispersion, low-information samples, feature-space choices, and upstream pipeline uncertainty?

## Central answer supported now

Within the frozen PRJNA1056765 Kraken2/Bracken pipeline, diagnosis accounts for only a small conditional variance component. Bray–Curtis evidence is dispersion-confounded, sparse taxon findings substantially overlap the original publication, and unsupervised community states are not stable across distance metrics. The manuscript contribution is therefore an inferential and reproducibility audit, not discovery of disease-specific microbiome fingerprints.

## Distinct contribution relative to Han et al.

- Quantify the diagnosis effect rather than equate significance with separation.
- Pair every PERMANOVA interpretation with dispersion testing.
- Distinguish all-available-cohort and pipeline-dependent QC-subset estimands.
- Separate compositional/statistical strengthening from upstream pipeline discrepancies.
- Report failure to identify stable ecotypes as a negative result.

## Explicit exclusions

No new taxon discovery, oral score, diagnostic model, mechanistic explanation, HUMAnN expansion, or external-dataset integration is part of the main story. Oral–lung ecology remains a future conditional branch only.

## Publication position

The strongest honest framing is a secondary analysis/reproducibility study showing that statistical significance in a large low-biomass BALF cohort does not imply strong diagnostic partitioning or natural community types. Likely strength is moderate if pipeline reconciliation is transparent; it becomes weak or no-go if presented as new disease biology.
